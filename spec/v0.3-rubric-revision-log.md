## Revision Rules & Governance Protocol

1. **Append-Only Structure:** Entries may be added to this log, but past entries may **NEVER** be deleted or retroactively altered.
2. **Mandatory Documentation Fields:** Every substantive rubric revision must specify:
   - **Revision Version & Date**
   - **Target Variable / Category**
   - **Previous Operational Definition**
   - **New Operational Definition**
   - **Motivating Evidence / Pilot Data Finding**
   - **Backward Compatibility Impact Statement**
3. **Major vs. Minor Revisions:**
   - **Minor Revision (e.g. v0.3.1):** Adding clarification examples to the Borderline-Case Library without changing category inclusion boundaries.
   - **Major Revision (e.g. v0.3.2 -> v0.4.0):** Altering category boundaries or adding/removing categories. Requires re-running calibration benchmarks.

## Log Entries

### Entry 001 — Version 0.3.0 Initial Baseline (2026-08-10)

* **Version:** `v0.3.0`
* **Author:** Larry Arnold / Cognitive Entropy Working Group
* **Scope:** Initial formal baseline for Decision Overhead ($D$) and Intent Deviation ($N$) observational rubrics.
* **Key Changes from Provisional Draft (MF-0.1):**
  1. Replaced causal classification `SYSTEM_IMPOSED` with observational category `SYSTEM_PRESENTED` for Decision Overhead ($D$).
  2. Introduced `DUAL_OR_CONTEXT_DEPENDENT` category for $D$ to account for system-mediated necessary choices (e.g. OAuth).
  3. Replaced causal classification `SYSTEM_INDUCED_DEVIATION` with observational category `INDIRECT_OR_DETOUR_BEHAVIOR` for Intent Deviation ($N$).
  4. Added `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` category to prevent forced ungrounded guesses.
  5. Established pre-session **Declared Task Intent ($I_{\text{task}}$)** prerequisite.
* **Motivating Evidence:** Epistemic design review highlighting that causal attributions cannot be directly observed from screen/telemetry recordings.
* **Backward Compatibility:** Replaces provisional uncalibrated definitions in MF-0.1.
